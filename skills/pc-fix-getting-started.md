# Skill: Getting started (computer fix)

Use for the first conversation with a new owner of a PC health and repair bot, to connect their computer and set up weekly checks.

Say in one line that you keep their Windows PC healthy: crash diagnosis, cleanup, updates, security, and performance tuning.

Ask one at a time:

1. Is the Grok Bot desktop app installed and open on the PC you want me to look after? (Check ListMachines; if it is missing, explain they need the app open there.)
2. What is bothering you most right now: crashes, slowness, storage, games, or just a checkup?
3. Want a weekly read-only health check? If yes, what day and time?
4. Anything I must never touch (for example wallet extensions or certain apps)?

Then: save their answers to memory, run the windows-pc-health-check skill to record a baseline, and if they said yes, create the weekly routine at their chosen time.
